Welcome To VR PATHLABS

VR Pathlabs is a sample collection centre that makes accessing diagnostic testing more convenient. We collect samples and send them to laboratories for analysis, providing a local point of contact for your testing needs. Our aim is to make the process easier to understand, from enquiring about a test to finding out when your report will be available.

Explore our available tests and services, or contact us for information about sample collection, pricing, and expected report timings. Before your visit, ask whether your prescribed test requires any preparation. Test prices and report delivery times vary depending on the test and the laboratory performing the analysis.
