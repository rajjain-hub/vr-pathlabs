About VR Pathlabs

Who We Are

VR Pathlabs is a sample collection centre in Indore, connecting visitors with laboratory testing services. We collect samples and send them to laboratories for analysis.

From enquiring about a test to accessing a report, we aim to make the process easier to understand. Visitors can explore tests and health checkup packages, request a booking, and contact us for assistance.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make accessing laboratory testing more convenient through clear information, accessible sample collection, and support throughout the process.
